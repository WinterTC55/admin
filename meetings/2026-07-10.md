# 4 Discussions

## 4.1 New meeting schedule

With the minimum common web API standard first edition published, it’s possible that TC55 is currently meeting more frequently than needed. The committee has decided to transition to monthly meetings on the last Friday. The timing is also less than ideal for colleagues in the Americas—James will create a poll to investigate availability across as many time zones as possible. This will be reevaluated in a few months.

Future meeting agendas will be started as soon as the previous meeting ends.

## 4.2 Increasing participation

Continuing from previous meetings—who isn’t attending and why not? Yes, it would be great if more runtimes were participating, but also framework developers.

Leo will reach out to NativeScript. Joe Sepi from Cloudflare is speaking in August about TC55. Chris will investigate what is keeping some current Ecma members from participating.

Owen pointed out that the WinterTC website could perhaps use some work in terms of guiding the curious toward active participation, and created [an issue](https://github.com/WinterTC55/admin/issues/200).

## 4.3 Kicking off common Node.js compat API

Recognizing that all runtimes at some point need to implement Node.js compatibility, James Snell [opened an issue](https://github.com/WinterTC55/admin/issues/195) to kick off a work item to investigate standardising that baseline. He plans to have a draft for review in the next week, so that delegates can review before the next meeting.

The initial draft will be intended to document reality without making an attempt to force a certain runtime to conform to anything that it hasn’t already implemented. The first question to tackle will be addressing what is the expected behaviour?

The committee agreed that this is something worth investigating further.

Given the fact that Node.js’s TSC isn’t likely to be receptive to giving control over to an outside group, Ethan floated the idea of WinterTC maintaining something adjacent to the WPT for node-compatible runtimes, though that may fall out of scope of the TC55 programme of work.

Owen was interested in providing a self-reporting platform for runtimes to confirm their compatibilities.
