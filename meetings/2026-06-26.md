# 4 Discussions

## 4.1 Committee chairs

As Andreu has mentioned, they will not be available starting in July to chair TC55 and at least one replacement will be needed to join Chris de Almeida (Open JS Foundation), who was elected on 28 April.

Due to Ecma’s request that the chair or a member of the chair group represent a non-NFP Ecma member, James Snell (Cloudflare) has volunteered to join the chair group until such a time that another delegate is in the position to hold that role. Ethan Arrowood was approved as a member of the chair group representing the Open JS Foundation pending his onboarding as an OpenJS delegate.

## 4.2 Effectiveness

### 4.2.1 Committee attendance

Low turnout for the committee continues to be a problem, despite clear interest from the community as evidenced by [Matrix channel](https://matrix.to/\#/) members and [Web Engines Hackfest breakout session](https://github.com/Igalia/webengineshackfest/issues/88) participation. Committee members will investigate holding their regular meeting at a different time to maximise participation.

### 4.2.2 Committee relevance

James Snell noted the lack of participation in TC55 specifically from most of the major runtimes, save Deno. He speculated that a lack of clearly defined workstreams may also have an impact on that lack of participation, and committed to working toward resolving this in the future.

James & Ethan will spend some time in the next month to clean up the existing proposals, repos, and open issues in the WinterTC55 GitHub org.

### 4.2.3 Potential topics of focus

#### 4.2.3.1 Web filesystems

One of the topics that came up at Web Engines Hackfest that is relevant to TC55’s work relates to filesystems. Owen referred to the informal conversation on [12 June](https://github.com/WinterTC55/admin/issues/191#issuecomment-4810224104) as a currently unsolved problem well within TC55’s scope.

#### 4.2.3.2 Node Compatibility

Another topic of conversation in A Coruña that has also been discussed by James Snell in the previous year is standardising APIs that are common Node.js APIs but are implemented across runtimes in the name of interoperability.

## 4.3 GitHub Projects

A common theme of the recent breakout session was that non-participants do not know how to bring an idea, proposal, or question to TC55. The admin GitHub repo has the appearance of being purely meta and members-only, and there’s no obvious intake. The secretary suggested perhaps the use of tooling along the lines of GitHub Projects for this purpose. Multiple delegates suggested perhaps having a GitHub repository specifically for the purpose of intake.

Owen Buckley opened a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/WinterTC55/admin/issues/197) to track this, and it will be discussed again at the next meeting.

The secretary suggested, in addition to a broad intake option, a specific intake for other TCs, SDOs, and consortia to communicate requests for liaisons or other interop-related engagement.
